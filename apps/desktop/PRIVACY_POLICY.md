Last updated: 2026-05-21

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to the desktop app in `apps/desktop` (Tauri-based).

## 3. Data processed by the app
Finanz Tracker processes and stores finance app data entered by you, including for example:
- users/profiles
- bookings (date, description, amount, category, account, note, tax flag)
- custom categories
- app settings
- optional sync configuration

## 4. Local storage and persistence
The desktop app stores data locally on your device (including persistent app state and settings).  
The project also includes local backup/export/import functionality.

## 5. Backup/export/import and folder sync
The app provides backup/export/import and folder-based sync workflows.  
When you use these features, files are read/written on paths/folders selected or configured by you.

## 6. Analytics, tracking, and crash reporting
The current desktop app project does not include third-party analytics SDKs or third-party crash-reporting SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics, Sentry, Mixpanel, etc.).

## 7. Network and external links
The app can open external links on user action from the Info section, including:
- email (`mailto:`)
- Linktree
- Telegram
- GitHub
- Buy Me a Coffee

When you open external links, the privacy policies of those third-party services apply.

### Third-party services referenced by links
- GitHub: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement
- Telegram: https://telegram.org/privacy
- Linktree: https://linktr.ee/s/privacy
- Buy Me a Coffee: https://www.buymeacoffee.com/privacy-policy

## 8. Data sharing
Based on the current implementation, the app does not automatically transmit your finance data to the developer.

If you use external sync services/tools, processing by those services/tools is governed by their own policies.

## 9. Backup/sync disclaimer
Backup/export/import/sync features are provided without guarantee of successful restore/sync in every environment.

You are responsible for verifying integrity and compatibility of backups and sync setups before relying on them.

## 10. Your choices
- You can use the app locally without enabling sync.
- You can choose whether to use backup/export/import features.
- You can avoid opening external links.

## 11. Changes to this policy
This policy may be updated if app functionality or legal requirements change.
